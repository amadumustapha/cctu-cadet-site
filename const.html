CCTU ARMY CADET CORPS

THE CONSTITUTION OF

CAPE COAST TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY ARMY CADET CORPS

Adopted: 10th August 2022 Legal Document

PREAMBLE

We the army cadet corps and the regiment band of Cape Coast Technical University hereby accept the following articles of this constitution as authoritatively binding to govern our corps trusting in God Almighty. We believe that this document shall foster unity and render a sense of common purpose among the members of army cadet corps of Cape Coast Technical University, also to gear effort to restore accountability through dignity and nobility.

We hereby adopt and accept this constitution on 10th August 2022 as a legal document for the government of the corps' administration and other activities.

ARTICLE 1: NAME

1. The corps shall be called, Cape Coast Technical University Army Cadet Corps and regimental band (CCTUACCRB)

2. The Motto of the corps shall be Loyalty, Discipline, Protect and Defend.

3. No person or authority shall raise any cadet corps except by or under the authority of parliament.

4. The cadet corps shall be equipped and maintained to perform its traditional functions of maintaining law and order in the university.

ARTICLE 2: AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

1. To promote good citizenship.

2. To stimulate interest in the land, sea, and air element of the country.

3. To promote patriotism.

4. To develop informed and responsible citizens.

5. To develop respect for constituted authority.

6. To develop a high degree of personal honor, self-reliance, individual discipline, and leadership.

7. To promote an understanding of the basic elements and need for national security.

8. To provide information on the military services as a responsible career.

9. To promote community service.

10. To promote secondary school completion.

ARTICLE 3: MEMBERSHIP

1. Membership form shall be purchased by any student at the Cape Coast Technical University who has an interest to join the corps.

2. There shall be a recruitment process to determine the fitness level of all potential members per military standards.

3. A written examination shall be conducted to determine which potential members qualifies per military standards.

4. There shall be a swearing of Oath to finally qualified members.

ARTICLE 4: MEMBERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Members shall be obliged to pay their subscription per semester.

2. Members must exhibit a high sense of discipline and respect during all activities.

3. Any member who purports to act for, or on behalf of the corps without the general consent of the Headquarters shall be held liable for his or her conduct.

4. Members shall be bound by the rules and regulations of the corps.

ARTICLE 5: MEMBERSHIP RIGHTS

1. The corps owns its obligation to defend it members when the need arises.

2. Liberty to use any available material of the corps through a recognized procedure with due care and diligence.

3. The right to report any misconduct of any member to the disciplinary committee

4. Right to information.

ARTICLE 7: TERM OF OFFICE

1. The term of office for the commander, the second in command and the adjutant shall be one academic year.

2. If as a result of any circumstance, a vacancy arises based on the advice of the disciplinary committee, there shall be, An appointment by Headquarters of the corps at an emergency durbar to appoint a member to fill that vacancy.

ARTICLE 8: FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICERS

The officers shall

1. Be responsible for the day to day running of the corps.

2. Report to members of the corps issues affecting the corps.

3. Draw up programs and activities for the corps subject to the approval of the members.

4. Publish a financial statement of the corps to members.

5. Work in all deliberation and in close harmony with the authorities of the cadet corps in matters affecting the interest of the members.

6. Consider the proposal of members where necessary.

7. Approve detailed transactions subject to the consent of simple majority at durbars.

8. Provide members of the corps a copy of the constitution.

ARTICLE 9: EVALUATION OF PERFORMANCE

1. There shall be a durbar dubbed "meet the officers" where the Headquarters brief members on their progress, fulfilment of promises, responsibilities, and short falls.

2. Members shall be required to ask the Headquarters constructive questions to clear unclarified questions or doubts concerning the corps and the officers.

3. This durbar shall be organized once at the middle of the semester.

4. The Headquarters of the corps shall be the organizer of the durbar.

5. The commanding officer shall chair this durbar and assume oversight on proceeding.

ARTICLE 10: FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICERS IN CHARGE

1. CADET MAJOR (C.O) shall:

a. To protect and defend the constitution of the corps, the institution, and the country.

b. To safeguard the internal security of the institution as directed by the higher authorities.

c. To organize, train and equip military knowledge to form vigilante group.

d. To uphold and advance the institution's policy, and interest of the students.

e. To conduct integrated parade during congregation.

f. Be a signatory to the bank account of the corps.

g. Perform any other duty assigned to him by the president.

2. CADET CAPTAIN (2IC) SHALL:

a. In the absence of the Commanding Officer, perform the functions stated under Article 10, section 1, sub-section (1), (3) and (5) except the formation of a committee.

b. Carry out special duties assigned to him or her by the Commanding Officer.

c. Assist the commander in the discharge of his duties.

d. Be the intelligence officer of the corps.

3. CADET LIEUTENANT (ADJUTANT) SHALL:

a. Keep proper records of all financial transactions of the corps.

b. Collect subscriptions, contributions, fines, record them and send them to the bank with some executives.

c. Be called to prepare statement of account cash, income and expenditure, balance sheet, etc.

d. Read financial statement at durbars where necessary.

e. Be a signatory to the bank account of the corps.

f. Be held liable for any financial misappropriation.

g. Keep the withdrawer book of the corps.

h. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

4. BAND MASTER

a. Shall be in charge of the activities of the regimental band.

b. Shall conduct integrated parade during congregation.

c. Shall advice the commander on issues concerning the regimental band.

d. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

5. DEPUTY BAND MASTER

a. Shall assume the power of the band master during his absence.

b. Shall assist the band master in the discharge of his duties.

c. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

6. BAND SECRETARY

a. Shall keep records of all cadet accoutrements.

b. Shall record minutes and keep accurate records of all meetings.

c. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

7. SENIOR ENSIGN

a. Shall be in charge of flags hoisting in the university.

b. Shall ensure effective use of the flags.

c. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

8. OPERATION OFFICER

a. Shall train recruits on basic military skills.

b. Shall prepare and modify operational strategies and plans.

c. Shall take charge by making sure that the right resources and tactics are used for each individual mission.

d. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

9. THE CADET WARRANT OFFICER I(CWOI)/CSM SHALL:

a. Maintains order and entails discipline among the other ranks or all ranks below him.

b. Organizes and makes sure the parade is ever ready for the Commander to carry on.

c. Serve as a link between the other ranks and the senior officers.

d. He dresses the parade for inspection, during ceremonial functions.

e. Perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

10. THE CADET WARRANT OFFICERS II(CWOII) shall:

a. Responsible for training the troops during endurance.

b. He makes sure that the corps are very strong enough to be on square.

c. Assist the C.S.M and serve as co drill instructor during drill time.

d. Ensure discipline among the other ranks and the officers.

e. Perform any duties assigned to them by the commander.

11. THE QUATERMASTER(CSSGT) shall:

a. Make general purchases together with the Commanding Officer.

b. Ensure effective use of all assets and its maintenance.

c. Keep records of all cadet accoutrements.

d. Keep in custody of all cadet accoutrements.

e. Oversees sharing of uniforms.

f. Perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

12. THE MESS OFFICER (CSSGT)

a. Shall perform and supervise the day activities of the mess, to benefit all members.

b. Shall promote and manage functions including planning, menus and costing in conjunction with the catering team.

c. Shall ensure standards of service detailed in the contract are achieved, maintained, and developed.

d. Shall perform any duties assigned to him by the commander.

ARTICLE 11: THE PATRONS

1. The dean of students' affairs shall be in charge of the cadet corps.

2. The commander shall report directly to the patron on welfare of the corps.

3. The instructor oversees everyday training and welfare of the cadet corps.

4. Time to time an instructor from our mother unit will be invited to train or give new drill to the cadet corps.

5. The patron shall direct the officers on security issues whenever the need arises.

6. The patron shall be the chief spokesperson of the cadet corps

7. The instructor shall take his allowances depending upon the number of times he reported on square in a semester.

ARTICLE 12: A. DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE

1. The disciplinary committee shall consist of ;

a. The patron who shall be the chairman.

b. The headquarters of the corps which comprises of the commander, second in command and the adjutant.

c. The provost.

B. CADET COUNCIL

1. There shall be established a cadet council which shall compose of;

a. The president who shall be the chairman

b. The cadet commander

c. The head of university security unit

d. The chairman of the judicial council.

e. The patron of the cadet corps.

f. Two (2) other officers appointed by the commander including one past officer.

ARTICLE 13: CONCERN

1. In the event of allegation, speculations and misconduct levelled against any member, the Headquarters shall require vivid evidence and exhibits its powers to ensure peace among members of the corps.

2. All members shall use correct channel of communication to seek re-dress to their consent.

ARTICLE 14: APPOINTMENT

1. The officers of the corps shall be appointed by the outgoing officers.

2. The Patron possesses the power to demote or sack any officer or member who goes against the constitution of the corps

3. A person who has spent three semesters in the cadet qualifies to be a commission officer.

4. The office of Commander, second in command, third in command and the C.S.M shall be given to a selfless person, who have served the corps and spent most of his time with the corps.

5. A student shall not be appointed as an officer if he does not have a sound mind [lunatic] or is detained as a criminal under any law enforced in Cape Coast Technical University or crime of offense under this constitution involving the security of the school, fraud, dishonesty, or moral turpitude.

6. Commissioned Officers shall not hold more than an office in the university.

7. Due to industrial attachment no member from the B-tech level 400 shall be a commissioned officer.

8. Appointed officers shall act in office until the next academic year resumes

ARTICLE 15: HANDING OVER

1. The outgoing officers shall handover to the incoming officers before the next academic year begins, witness by the members of the corps.

2. Officers shall arrange and organize the handing over parade in consultation with the instructor.

3. Appointed officers shall act in office until the next academic year resumes.

ARTICLE 16: SOURCE OF FUNDS

The main sources of funds for the corps shall be from:

1. Yearly subscriptions.

2. Dues per semester.

3. Voluntary.

4. Activities organized by the Institution.

5. Donation by benevolent organization, etc.

ARTICLE 17: DURBAR

1. There shall be a general durbar as the need arises.

2. Such durbars of the corps shall be held on Wednesdays within the week.

3. There shall be an emergency durbar when the need arises.

4. There shall be quorum where members present form two third of the membership at durbar.

ARTICLE 18: CONDUCT OF DURBAR

1. The cadet sergeant major (CSM) being the speaker, shall direct his/her speech directly to the point on the agenda under discussion.

2. The Commanding officer (C.O) shall interpret the standing order, subject to the approval of the house. He /She shall first take part in all discussions.

3. There shall no point of order during any order business.

4. The commanding officer cannot be put under any order.

ARTICLE 19: CODE OF CONDUCT

1. If any member challenges an officer will face a serious punishment or possibly demoted from his rank.

2. Any member or an officer who absent himself from training, durbar or any ceremonial program shall be punished and suspended at the same time.

3. Any officer who leaves his beard unshaved shall be sanctioned.

4. Any member or an officer who put on Camo or O.G to any program without the approval of the Headquarters shall be charged.

5. Any officer who punishes members with no reason shall be sanction.

6. Any member or an officer found stealing logistics from the corps or from the Institution shall be sacked from the corps.

7. Any member or an officer found smoking shall be sanctioned.

8. Any member who fails to pay compliment to officers shall be punish.

9. Any officer who misplaces cadet accoutrements shall purchase it.

ARTICLE 21: ORDER OF PRECEENCE

1. Commanding officer shall allow the following procedural motion to be put when there is already a proposition on the table, in the following order of precedence:

◦ That the motion not to be put now.

◦ The motion to be put now.

◦ Motion to be postponed or adjourn the matter to a later specified durbar.

◦ Next business.

◦ The matter to lie on the table.

◦ A motion to adjourn the durbar to another date.

2. All procedural motion shall receive a majority vote casting including abstentions.

3. If the motion "That the motion be put now" has been suspended, no further discussion of original question shall be allowed, but a member may give notice of his/her desire carried, only that the original motion is putting into vote.

4. If the motion "limit a debate to a specific time period" is carried, the Commanding officer shall draw up a list of those wishing to make their specific period. The proposer shall be allowed a maximum of (5) five minute to sum up before the motion is put to vote.

ARTICLE 22: BANKING

The corps shall open an account with CBG Bank.

The signatories to the account shall comprise:

1. The Dean of Student Affairs as the Principal.

2. The Commanding Officer.

3. The Adjutant.

ARTICLE 23: AUDITING

1. The Headquarters shall avail itself for auditing purposes as when the institution demands.

2. The Headquarters shall report directly to the patrons on the financial uses of the corps

3. Annual financial report shall be submitted to the house at the end of the second semester of the office of the officers.

4. The report from above shall be read and approved by the house at a durbar

ARTICLE 24: DISCIPLINE

1. Discipline will be meted against any member who flouts any of the rules of the corps.

2. Any officer who absents himself from training and meeting for three consecutive sitting shall lose his incentives as an officer.

3. Any member or officer who mishandles the corps assets shall be held responsible.

4. Any impeached member or officer shall have only 24 hours to hand over all the corps documents in his possession.

5. No officer shall keep any cadet accoutrements for more than seven days.

6. Quartermaster shall take approval from the commander before releasing any cadet accoutrements.

7. Past officers shall audit the cadet accoutrements whenever the need arises.

8. In case of established embezzlement, the culprit(s) shall be given fourteen (14) days within which to refund all monies and failure to pay the embezzled amount within the specified period, they shall be handed to the University authorities and finally handed over to the police.

9. The cadet officers shall allocate fifty percent (50) of the cadet component on the implementation of cadet project.

ARTICLE 25: RESIGNATION

1. An Officer may resign from his or her post, upon a written notice to the house and this will be read and discussed by Headquarters.

2. The Officer concerned will be required to handover all documents belonging to the corps which is in his/her possession at the time of resignation.

3. An Officer may be removed from office by the Headquarters and in a durbar, called for that purpose from any of the following:

• Proven inefficient

• Proven corrupt

• Gross misconduct or neglect of duty likely to embarrass the corps and, or otherwise put the welfare of the corps and school to jeopardy.

ARTICLE 26: AMENDMENTS AND REVISION

1. Any article, clause or section of this constitution may be altered or otherwise amended by a Five members committee appointed by the Headquarters for that purpose.

2. The resolution of the amendment should be carried out by a two –third majority of members present by voting for that purpose.

3. The five members committee shall have a preliminary hearing on the proposal for amendment and where it is satisfied that conditions require an amendment of a portion of this constitution, shall be given permission for amendment.

B. REVISION

1. Any Member shall petition the headquarters for a total Revision of this constitution where it has been established that.

a. The constitution has not been reviewed for at least the past five years.

b. Subject to clause (a) of this article a need has risen for the constitution to be reviewed to suit current development.

C. PROHIBITION AND ALTERATION

1. Unless where constitutional amendment has been Made to some provisions of this constitution no part No part thereof shall be altered or excluded or any Additions to its content.

2. Any person who;

a. Alters, exclude, or makes addition to any part thereof

i. of this constitution without being sanction by law shall

ii. be held to have committed an offence to this

iii. constitution.

b. In the case of an Officer shall be a valid ground for

i. removal from office.

VOTING

1. Voting shall be by members only of which each accredited member shall be entitled to one voting.

2. Voting privilege shall include the right of members to second motions or nomination.

3. In voting motion shall be carried by a simple majority of members present.

4. The Commanding officer shall exercise the casting of vote.
