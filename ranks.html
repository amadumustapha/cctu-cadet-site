CCTU ARMY CADET CORPS

Cadet Ranks & Appointments

Complete hierarchy of the CCTU Army Cadet Corps command structure

24 Ranks & Appointments
COMMANDING OFFICER

CADET MAJOR

Chief executive cadet officer in charge of overall command, strategic decisions, and unit operational readiness.

SECOND IN COMMAND

CADET CAPTAIN

Deputy commander managing administrative affairs, logistical support, and assisting the CO in command.

ADJUTANT

CADET LIEUTENANT

Third in command, responsible for administrative duties, record keeping, and financial transactions.

SENIOR COLOUR ENSIGN

CADET LIEUTENANT

Responsible for the corps flags, colours, and ceremonial flag hoisting duties.

JUNIOR COLOUR ENSIGN

CADET LIEUTENANT

Assists the Senior Colour Ensign in managing flags, colours, and ceremonial duties.

JUNIOR ENSIGN

CADET LIEUTENANT

Supports colour party duties and assists in flag-related ceremonial functions.

SENIOR OPERATIONS OFFICER

CADET 2ND LIEUTENANT

Plans and coordinates operational strategies, training programs, and field exercises.

DEPUTY OPERATIONS OFFICER

CADET 2ND LIEUTENANT

Assists the Senior Operations Officer in planning and executing operational activities.

CADET SERGEANT MAJOR

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS I

Senior NCO responsible for discipline, drill instruction, and parade ground management.

DEPUTY CADET SERGEANT MAJOR

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS I

Assists the Cadet Sergeant Major in maintaining discipline and drill instruction.

PHYSICAL TRAINING INSTRUCTOR

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Enforces combat physical fitness, stamina runs, obstacle conditioning, and leads body conditioning drills.

DEPUTY PHYSICAL TRAINING INSTRUCTOR

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Assists the PTI in physical training sessions and fitness assessments.

CADET PROVOST

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Enforces discipline, handles internal security matters, and manages cadet conduct.

DEPUTY PROVOST

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Assists the Provost in maintaining order and discipline among cadets.

SENIOR COLOUR ESCORT

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Leads the colour party and ensures proper handling of flags during ceremonies.

DEPUTY COLOUR ESCORT

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS II

Assists the Senior Colour Escort in colour party duties and ceremonial flag handling.

QUARTER MASTER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Manages cadet accoutrements, uniforms, and equipment inventory.

DEPUTY QUARTER MASTER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Assists the Quarter Master in managing equipment and uniform distribution.

MESS OFFICER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Supervises mess activities, planning menus, and managing catering functions.

DEPUTY MESS OFFICER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Assists the Mess Officer in managing mess activities and catering services.

FIRST AIDER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Provides first aid and medical support during training and ceremonial activities.

DEPUTY FIRST AIDER

CADET STAFF SERGEANT

Assists the First Aider in providing medical support and first aid services.

PUBLIC RELATIONS OFFICER / MEDIA

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS I

Manages public relations, media coverage, and promotes the corps image.

DEPUTY P.R.O / MEDIA

CADET WARRANT OFFICER CLASS I

Assists the PRO in managing public relations, communications, and media activities.
