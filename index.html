About University Cadet Corps

Discipline, Patriotism, and Leadership Excellence

Our Mission

To develop disciplined and patriotic citizens who defend the Constitution, serve their communities, and uphold the unity and progress of Ghana. We foster selfless leadership, integrity, and a deep sense of civic duty in every cadet. Through rigorous training and mentorship, we instill respect for human dignity, justice, and the rule of law.

Our Vision

A Ghana where every young citizen is empowered, responsible, and deeply committed to the ideals of freedom, justice, probity, and accountability. We envision a generation of cadets who are morally grounded, physically prepared, and actively engaged in nation-building. Our cadets recognize that their rights are inseparable from their duties to the state and their fellow citizens.
